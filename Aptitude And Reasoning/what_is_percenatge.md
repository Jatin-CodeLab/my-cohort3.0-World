# What is Percentage?

Percentage ka matlab hota hai: **"Per Hundred"**, yani **100 me se kitna**.

- 100 Percentage me se kitna hissa.
- Percentage ko **%** Symbol se likhte hai.

### Example

Agar kisi class me **100 Student** hai aur **80% students pass** ho gaye, to:

- Pass Student = **80%**
- Fail Student = **20%**

> Yani 100 Student me se 80 Student pass.

> "In mathematics, a percentage is a ratio or fraction that can be expressed as a number out of 100."

# Trick - 1

We need to take out the percentage of a specific number.

### Formula

```text
(value × percentage) / 100
```

### Example

```text
10% of 80

20% of 150
```

### 1)

Kabhi bhi kisi bhi number ka **10%** nikalna ho to us number ka **last digit ko point ke piche le jao**.

#### Example

```text
80 ka 10% = 8

10% of 150 = 15

10% of 1569 = 156.9
```

### 2)

Aur kabhi agar aise numbers ho jisme **10% na ho**, jaise **12, 13, 56**, uske liye:

#### Example

```text
15% of 500 = 75

12% of 728 = 87.36

19% of 92761 = 17624.56
```
